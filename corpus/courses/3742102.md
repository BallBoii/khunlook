## Description
Exercise as a part of treatment modalities; definition of physical activity and exercise; basic anatomy and physiology; measurement of physical fitness; types of exercise; guidelines for exercise prescription; exercise for prevention and treatment of non-infectious chronic diseases: osteoarthritis, obteoporosis, type2 diabetes, metabolic syndrome, hypertension, and cardiovascular diseases.
