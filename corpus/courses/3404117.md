## Description
Introduction to the study of laws; nature and sources of law, kinds and characteristics of law; the making, application, and interpretation of law and its abrogation; law of persons: natural and juristic persons; legal rights; nature and kinds of rights; rights under the Civil and Commercial Codes; other laws having economic implication, including antitrust laws and consumer protection law. (Designed for students of Arts)
